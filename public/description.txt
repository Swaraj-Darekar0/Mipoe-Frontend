SECTION 4 — FAQ
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

=== BRAND FAQ — 5 Q&As ===

Q1: Do I need a big budget to start?
A: No. You can launch a campaign starting from ₹5,000. Our CPV model means
   you only spend money when real viewers actually watch your content — not
   just when an ad loads on someone's screen. Start small, scale what works.

Q2: How is this different from running Instagram or YouTube ads?
A: Ads are push. sellr is pull. When a creator who already has a trusting
   audience talks about your product — that's earned media, not paid media.
   Our data shows 3–6x higher conversion rates vs. display ads in the same
   category, because the recommendation comes from a real person, not a brand.

Q3: What types of campaigns can I run?
A: Three types — and you can mix them:
   → UGC Video Campaigns: creators make authentic content about your product.
   → Affiliate Campaigns: creators drive sales; you pay per conversion.
   → Clipping Campaigns: creators cut and post viral-format short clips of your
     existing video content, expanding your reach with zero extra production cost.
   One dashboard. Three growth levers.

Q4: How do I know the views are real and not bots?
A: Every view is verified through the platform's engagement tracking before
   any payout is processed. Creators only get paid for verified organic reach.
   That means your billing is 100% tied to real human eyeballs — not inflated
   impressions bought off an exchange.

Q5: How long until I see results?
A: Most campaigns get their first creator submissions within 48 hours of going
   live. Campaigns with a clear brief and competitive CPV are typically fully
   staffed within 72 hours. You'll see real-time data on views and conversions
   from day one — no waiting for a monthly agency report.

---

=== CREATOR FAQ — 5 Q&As ===

Q1: Do I need to be a big influencer to join?
A: No. This is literally the point of sellr. If your content is good and your
   audience trusts you — even 500 followers — there are brands here that want
   to work with you. The era of "wait for a DM from a marketing agency" is
   over. You come here, browse live campaigns, and apply directly.

Q2: How exactly do I get paid?
A: Three ways, depending on campaign type:
   → CPV Payout: you earn per verified view on your campaign content.
   → Affiliate Commission: you earn a % of every sale driven by your unique link.
   → UGC Flat Fee: brands pay a fixed rate for the content you create,
     regardless of views — think of it as a brand content job.
   Payouts go directly to your linked payout account after verification.

Q3: Do I have to follow a brand script?
A: Brands on sellr specifically want authentic content — not scripted ads.
   They give you a brief (product, key message, do's and don'ts), and you
   create in your own style, your own language, your own voice. That's the
   whole reason creators on this platform convert better than traditional ads.

Q4: What if I post content and it doesn't get many views?
A: Your payout scales with your reach — so lower views means lower CPV payout,
   but you never go into debt or get penalized. The risk is on the brand, not
   on you. For UGC campaigns (flat fee), you get paid regardless of view count.
   Build your audience and your earnings grow with it.

Q5: Can I participate in multiple campaigns at the same time?
A: Yes — absolutely. Browse active campaigns, apply to as many as fit your
   content style, and run them simultaneously. Your sellr creator profile
   tracks all active campaigns, earnings, and payouts in one place. More
   campaigns = more income streams, simple.

SECTION 9 — TONE & VOICE GUIDELINES (For All Copywriting)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

DO:
→ Speak like a smart friend, not a pitch deck.
→ Lead with what the user gets, not what the platform does.
→ Use numbers when they add credibility (3–6x ROAS, ₹0.08 CPV, 200M creators).
→ India-first framing — "creator economy," "India's creators," "every city."
→ Short sentences. Punchy. One idea per line.

DON'T:
→ Say "innovative," "cutting-edge," "revolutionary," "disruptive."
→ Use passive voice ("brands are connected with creators").
→ Bury the lead — put the benefit in the headline, not the 3rd paragraph.
→ Make financial promises without a disclaimer.
→ Use jargon the target user (a 22-year-old creator in Pune) wouldn't know.

TARGET USERS:
  Creator: 18–28, India, any city, 500–500K followers, wants income from content.
  Brand: 25–45, DTC/SME/startup founder, tired of wasting ad spend.
