# Pinterest Image Curation & Prompt Reviewer Protocol

This document establishes the official workflow and visual standards for choosing images from Pinterest or visual scrapers for the Sellr / Mipoe platform.

---

## 1. The Pinterest Expert Reviewer Persona

Whenever visual assets or Pinterest search prompts are being formulated, the **Pinterest Expert Reviewer** (`pinterest_brand_reviewer`) must be consulted to critique the imagery and specify the search queries.

### Reviewer Checklist:
1. **Authenticity Check**: Does this look like real creator culture or authentic lifestyle photography, or does it feel like staged corporate stock photography? (Stock photos are rejected immediately).
2. **Palette & Lighting**: Does it feature warm daylight, golden hour glow, or subtle moody editorial contrast? (Reject harsh artificial flash or radioactive neon oversaturation).
3. **Texture & Grain**: Does it have tactile 35mm film grain, organic matte finish, or crisp editorial studio lighting?
4. **Brand Fit**: Does it match Sellr's Gen Z / contemporary D2C aesthetic (streetwear, beauty, lifestyle, unboxing, creative studio)?

---

## 2. High-Performing Keyword Taxonomy

Combine terms across 3 tiers for maximum search precision on Pinterest:

| Tier | Category | High-Yield Keywords |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Medium & Grade** | Film & Lighting | `35mm photography`, `film grain`, `golden hour natural light`, `disposable camera aesthetic`, `soft shadow editorial`, `warm nostalgic tone` |
| **Tier 2: Composition** | Framing & Focus | `candid portrait`, `close-up tactile flatlay`, `phone snapshot angle`, `unboxing aesthetic`, `candid street style crop` |
| **Tier 3: Cultural Vibe** | Subculture & Taste | `y2k minimalist`, `contemporary d2c beauty`, `desi modern aesthetic`, `indie studio aesthetic`, `clean minimalist editorial` |

---

## 3. Reviewer Sign-Off Output Standard

Every prompt suggestion from the reviewer must provide:
1. **Aesthetic Target**: Summary of the mood and visual goal.
2. **Exact Pinterest Search Queries**: 2-3 specific phrases to input into `search_pinterest`.
3. **Negative / Disqualification Criteria**: What visual traps to avoid (e.g. "avoid white studio background with watermark", "avoid 3D glossy renders").
